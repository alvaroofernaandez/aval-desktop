# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Expected: being respectful of differing viewpoints, giving and accepting
constructive feedback, focusing on what is best for the community.

Not acceptable: harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or
political attacks, publishing others' private information without permission,
and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that violate this code, and may ban contributors
temporarily or permanently. Report incidents privately through
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://github.com/alvaroofernaandez/aval-desktop/security/advisories/new)
or to the maintainer directly.
